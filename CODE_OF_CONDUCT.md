# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on ideas and implementation, not individuals.
- Assume good intent and collaborate professionally.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language
- Personal attacks or intimidation
- Sharing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation for behavior that violates this policy.

## Reporting

Report incidents to: maintainers@localhost.invalid
